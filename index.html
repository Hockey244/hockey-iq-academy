Meet CJ

CJ

Founder of Hockey IQ Academy

Student of the Game • Hockey IQ Enthusiast • Video Breakdown Creator

I'm a young hockey player who's always been fascinated by what separates the best NHL players in the world—players like Connor McDavid, Cale Makar, Nathan MacKinnon, Quinn Hughes, Sidney Crosby, Nikita Kucherov, and Leon Draisaitl—from everyone else.

After practices and games, I'd go home and watch NHL highlights for hours. But I wasn't just watching the goals. I'd pause the videos, slow them down, replay the same shifts over and over again, and study every little detail.

I wasn't asking, "How did he score?" I was asking why the best players made the decisions they did.

The more I studied them, the more I realized they weren't just more skilled—they understood the game differently. They recognized patterns, anticipated plays, created time and space, and consistently made great decisions under pressure.

That realization inspired me to create Hockey IQ Academy, where I help players develop the Hockey IQ needed to understand the game, recognize patterns, and make smarter decisions every time they step onto the ice.
