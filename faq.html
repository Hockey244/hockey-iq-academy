Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hockey IQ Academy memberships, courses, NHL breakdowns, and improving your hockey IQ.

What is Hockey IQ Academy?

Hockey IQ Academy is an online learning platform designed to help players improve their hockey IQ through video breakdowns, courses, and real NHL examples.

Who are the courses for?

Our content is built for players who want to understand the game at a deeper level, whether you're just starting out or already competing at a high level.

How do memberships work?

Memberships unlock premium Hockey IQ content, including exclusive courses, NHL breakdowns, and new educational resources as they are released.

How often is new content added?

New videos, breakdowns, and educational content will be added regularly so members always have something new to study.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. Memberships can be cancelled at any time.
